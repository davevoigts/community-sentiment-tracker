How scores and weights are calculated
  1. Each item is split into sentences, and sentences are split at "but", "however" and similar, so mixed views are scored part by part.
  2. Each sentence is scored from -1 to +1 by .
  3. Word effects: each word is removed in turn and the sentence re-scored. The difference is that word's measured effect. Green words pushed the score up, red pushed it down. This includes rules such as "not" flipping the next word.
  4. A sentence counts toward a theme when it contains one of the theme's keywords. An item's theme score is the mean of those sentences.
  5. An item's overall score is the mean of all its sentences, so long articles don't swing to the extremes.
  6. Weekly figure = sum(item score x source weight) / sum(weights). Each item counts once. Its share is shown on each item.
  7. 90% range: items are resampled 1,000 times and the middle 90% of the results is reported. Overlapping ranges between weeks mean the change could be noise.
